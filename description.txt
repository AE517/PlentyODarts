PlentyODarts is a small and simple mod that aims to bring a new way of enjoying the Throwing Class, without feeling out of place.
It introduces the Dart Master Class, a Damage Class that uses, well, Darts! Specifically Hand Darts.

Small and precise this pointy weapons bring a whole new way of playing by exploiting enemies defenses and bypassing their immunities.

Create Darts or find them in the world, learn about the Dart Masters Clan and unveil the countless combinations that Modus and Shifts can bring...

All that and more...
So... Are you ready to hit the Big Fish?

PlentyODarts uses the Particle Library as a dependency.
This amazing mod made by SnowyStarfall can be found at https://github.com/SnowyStarfall/ParticleLibrary/tree/main.
